About Me

BORN TO FLY

Aiswarya Chakravorty is a clinical psychologist with a passion for research and teaching. She has a B.Sc., M.Sc., and M.Phil. in Psychology from Calcutta University and The West Bengal University of Health Sciences. She is a former clinical psychologist of NIMHANS, Bangalore and currently attached with Caluctta National Medical College. She has conducted research on topics such as depression, body image, emotional regulation, coping style, meaning of life, and personality traits. She has also worked as a psychologist and a guest lecturer in various organizations, including NAMAH Foundation, National Institute of Behavioral Sciences, West Bengal State University, Ruby General Hospital, and National Institute of Mental Health and Neuro-Sciences. She is currently working as a part-time clinical psychologist at Mind Mentor Clinic at Garia, Care & Cure Clinic at Jodhpur Park and Pulse Diagnostics at Sarat Bose Road. She has participated in several workshops and seminars on clinical psychology and has received an award for securing the highest marks in Psychology Honors examination in 2016. She is fluent in English and Bengali and enjoys reading, writing, and traveling.
